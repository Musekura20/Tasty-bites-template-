Fluffy Pancakes in 15 Minutes

ME

Musekura

Oct 1, 2026   4 min read

Good pancakes do not need a special recipe. You only need simple ingredients and a little patience. Follow these steps and your breakfast will be ready in 15 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 spoons of sugar
  • 1 spoon of baking powder

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the milk and egg. Stir until the mix is smooth.
  3. Heat a pan on medium heat and add a little oil.
  4. Pour one spoon of mix and wait until you see small bubbles.
  5. Flip the pancake and cook for one more minute.
Tip: Do not over-mix. A few small lumps make the pancakes softer.
